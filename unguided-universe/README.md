# Unguided Universe

![License](https://img.shields.io/badge/License-MIT%20%2B%20CC%20BY--NC--SA%204.0-blue.svg)

> _A persistent MMO 4X where a band of lost captains (you) navigate an unguided universe_

A turn-based spatial MMO project where survival depends on improvisation. Play as a ship captain with your crew, exploring hostile systems where intelligent life can only survive on space stations and vessels. The mystery? Nobody knows why the planets became uninhabitable, and nobody knows how to get home.

## 🎯 **Core Vision**

**Setting:** Space systems have become hostile to intelligent life. Mysterious viruses, "cephalo-devourers" and strange phenomena have made planets uninhabitable. Intelligent races survive on space stations, ships and platforms.

**Gameplay:** Simultaneous turn-based combat inspired by board game mechanics. 2-minute turns with secret move/action planning and priority-based resolution.

**Scale:** Designed for 10,000+ _(but it would be cool to have at least 2 players)_ simultaneous players in a persistent universe fighting in 3D ~~grid~~ cubes.
